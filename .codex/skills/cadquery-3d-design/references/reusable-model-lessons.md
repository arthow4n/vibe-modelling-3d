# Reusable model evidence

During planning, check for a similar interface or manufacturing problem here,
then read the linked object's current source and evidence. Reuse a successful
builder when practical. Preserve its mating geometry and print orientation;
reassess movement, loads, stiffness or printing where the new assembly changes
the relevant conditions. A fit or print result applies to the tested
configuration, not every material or printer.

The revised head-up screw and final plate were reported to print nicely. | The print report does not establish load capacity or long-term creep. Recalculate the load path when loads, geometry or supports differ; review the drive socket orientation and thread interface where changed. |
| Minimal gravity postcard support | [Wave postcard display](../../../../model/postcard_display/README.md#print-status): the user reports the full Wave holder was printed and works well. The report does not validate the other variants or every card size/orientation. |
| Analysis-driven flexible catch | [Phone stand API experiment](../../../../model/analysis_phone_stand/README.md#what-using-the-api-changed): prescribed release and local holding-contact solves informed a wider root and positive travel stop; force was stable across the recorded refinement studies. | Unprinted, solid PETG assumptions. Local peak strain was mesh sensitive; sharp-tooth pass-over failed. Reuse the analysis pattern, not the numerical load rating or material limit. |
| Known snap, flexure and structural questions | [Shared engineering questions](../../../../physical_analysis/README.md#use) construct cases, check quality and plan bounded studies from explicit geometry/regions. Sliding snap and phone release reproduce their native results; the book plain-back cross-check agrees within 6%; lift-off contact remains rejected. | Historical backend identity stays visible. Sliding peak force remains increment-sensitive; the book L face-load case distorts more than its beam approximation. Numerical/material/manufacturing/physical evidence remain separate. |
| Rounded sliding snap with in-layer flexure | [Twenty-swatch box](../../../../model/filament_swatch_box/README.md#engineering-evidence): contact drives engagement and reopening in one elastic solve; mesh/penalty studies, increment sensitivity and actual effectively solid snap paths are retained. A thin rail stem and insufficient head relief were corrected using slice/CAD evidence. | Unprinted, frictionless isotropic PETG assumption with a local clamped root. Guide play changes estimated detent force, and travel-increment sensitivity dominated force precision despite stable mesh/penalty results. Retention acceptance and complete deformed-beam clearance remain unresolved. Printed friction, bonding, fatigue and set remain uncalibrated; do not transfer numerical limits as material data. |
| Covered press-release snap and sliding contact | [Lift-off box](../../../../model/filament_swatch_lift_box/README.md#evidence-and-remaining-uncertainty): actual contact drivers exposed a blocked finger/cap path, artificial actuator side restraint and a window ledge needing a release lead. [Controlled diagnosis](../../../../model/filament_swatch_lift_box/notes/debug/README.md) led to guarded saved-mesh reuse and saved-field contact localization. | Unprinted. Surface-to-surface pairing is fixed per increment, not renewed each iteration; identical requested mesh settings need not give identical node positions. Fixed-mesh travel controls still fail contact quality. Reuse fixture checks, controlled-study and diagnostic tools, not failed force/strain predictions or assumed PETG limits. Failure does not establish impossible geometry or an incapable backend. Large native result extraction is streamed and recoverable without another solve. |
| Deep protective cap over a browsing file | [Upright swatch file](../../../../model/filament_swatch_upright_box/README.md#print-plan-and-evidence): an outboard overlapping shield preserves card space and separates the mechanism opening from contents. Actual Orca paths exposed omitted catch support; disabling small-overhang suppression restored it. | Unprinted. Do not universally disable suppression: inspect consequential overhangs and removal access in the actual slice. Support gaps/solidity do not establish adhesion or material properties. Local geometric identity does not establish whole-shell compliance, comfort, retention or solve the inherited contact-quality limitation. |

Add or revise an entry autonomously when a printed result, measured model check,
or repeatable failure can save work on another object. Link to the current
object record and source, state what was actually observed and under which known
setup, and name the boundary of transfer. Keep detailed measurements and trial
history in the object directory. Remove or correct an entry when later evidence
supersedes it; do not append session narratives or untested clearance rules.
