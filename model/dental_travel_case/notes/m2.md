# M2B — retention redesign and computational investigation

**Continuation:** [M3](m3.md) adapts the investigation to a practical reduced
model and delivers a new prototype with paired exports and cross-section SVGs.
This M2B record preserves the earlier full-contact limitations and research state.

M1 is the **failed printed negative reference**, frozen with its original source,
exports and [physical observations](m1.md#full-box-print-feedback--2026-10-15).
The user authorized a separate computational redesign on 2026-10-17. M2B is an
**unprinted research candidate, not a qualified travel closure**. No new print is
requested. Its longer guide and shorter leaves improve specific geometric and
traction responses; a defensible full-assembly opening barrier is still unresolved.
The precise microscopic escape route of the previous print has not been measured.

**Decision, 2026-10-18:** stop this investigation short of retention qualification.
Both direct full-assembly routes exhausted host memory. The lower-memory native
route passed its small controls, but failed the initial linear solve on the actual
joint at two ILUT fill levels. Further solver/preconditioner development or a
separately qualified structural representation is needed before a full-case
opening barrier can be predicted. M2B is retained for research, with no new
print-ready exports or print recommendation. Neither requested outcome is fully
achieved: the candidate is improved in specific checked quantities, and the
workflow detects weaker evidence, but no qualified pull-off predictor exists.


## Current candidate and product consequences

[M2B authoritative CAD](../m2b_design.py) preserves the two-cup architecture,
264 mm length, 51.2 mm depth, 74.2 mm main width and minimum contents aperture.
The sleeve grows from 12 to 30 mm, with the cap receiving band extending beyond
its effective bearing span. Local receiver reinforcement increases maximum width
to **75.8 mm**. That is an explicit compromise: the earlier exact 25 mm overall
width reduction is not met, and the verification plan retains its FAIL.

Flat retaining lands widen from 8 to 9 mm; their radial flat depth remains 1.5 mm.
The receiving skin grows from .8 to 1.3 mm including an external .8 mm buttress.
Tooth-to-pocket outer clearance increases from .15 to .45 mm, so the .25 mm guide
play does not preload the leaf in the same tested adverse pose. These dimensions
are geometry hypotheses, not calibrated printer compensation. The cantilevers
remain 1.6 mm thick but shorten from 40.4 to 32 mm. Controls rise 4 mm, remaining
recessed; their guard ports open to the seam so the old roof does not obstruct
travel. The 1.6 mm space from pad top to cap rim requires actual finger-access
assessment; M1 access evidence does not transfer automatically.

Closing requires both catches to recover after seating; deliberate opening uses
both recessed pads with the joint unloaded. CAD samples examine this path, but
press comfort, overtravel, printed recovery, creep and wear remain unknown.
The longer sleeve retains the minimum aperture but changes how far contents must
pass through it. Individual brush/tube proxies clear with .8 mm modeled clearance;
combined easy loading is not established. The printed M1 required force to load.
Existing [S16 liners](../../dental_travel_case_liner/README.md) and old print layouts
are **not qualified for M2B**. No liner or accessory compatibility is inherited.

## What the baseline actually establishes

The investigation uses the actual current [M1 CAD](../m1_design.py), not a
reconstructed cartoon. [Geometric evidence](retention/m1_geometry.json) and
[reproducer](../retention_geometry.py) distinguish flat face contact from rounded
corner obstruction. Both catches have correctly opposed axial flat lands:
**1.50 × 8 = 12 mm² each**. Rounded material still obstructs withdrawal after
1.5 mm local retraction, until approximately 1.75 mm. A binary intersection
therefore overstates useful flat retaining engagement.

M1 nominal tooth/pocket exterior clearance is .15 mm, smaller than .25 mm guide
play. In the tested adverse lateral pose the receiver requires approximately
.103 mm inward leaf displacement just to seat. This reduces available margin;
it does not establish that the printed catches never engaged. No tested admissible
rigid pose releases both catches. Receiver movement, deformation, manufacturing
variation and contact migration must remain in the explanation.

Axial load is eccentric to the leaf neutral axis. Actual-CAD nonlinear traction
experiments demonstrate **inward bending despite initially flat retaining faces**.
At 12 N on the exact 12 mm² receiving land, E=1200 MPa, mean shoulder retraction
is 1.0736 mm. Varying E from 800 to 2200 MPa gives 1.2750 to .7659 mm without
fitting properties to the print. A finer curved mesh changes displacement by
about .3%, but local peak strain changes by more than 10%; displacement is the
stronger supported result.

The [deformed-face witness](retention/m1_deformed_face.json), reconstructed from
quadratic native nodal fields by [this script](../retention_face_witness.py), gives
approximately **4.23° inward-camming slope** at the loaded shoulder. A normal
reaction there would have inward/axial component ratio .074. The fixed-world
traction experiment does not apply that feedback or follow the receiver around
the corner. It is a demonstrated weakening mechanism, **not a measured or
predicted pull-off force**. It does not prove that this was the actual print's
sole escape route. Printed dimensions, modulus, orientation and exact artifact
were not supplied; neither friction nor strength has been calibrated.

The old checks missed the distinction between deliberate release effort,
nominal capture and the force/work barrier of the whole deformable mechanism.
The full-box physical failure overrides their apparent digital success. A cropped
joint can characterize a local leaf but cannot establish long-shell behavior.

### Deliberate operation: fixture matters

[Flat-pad displacement study](retention/dental_m2b_release/result.json) constrains
all selected pad nodes to identical radial travel. Its 1.35 mm pad movement
requires 16.75 N and produces only about 1.54 mm tip travel, with 3.11% local
peak strain near the pad. That constraint suppresses pad rotation; it represents
a stiff flat pressing fixture, not automatically a conforming finger. It is kept
as a counterexample to interpreting prescribed travel as a realistic human press.

[Uniform pad force](retention/dental_m2b_press_force/result.json) permits rotation.
At 3.6 N, E1200, isolated root stub, mean pad travel is 1.288 mm, tip 2.563 mm,
and minimum shoulder travel 2.109 mm. Peak strain is .753%; this single result
is not a strain qualification. The [4.2 N numerical study](retention/dental_m2b_press_study/study_result.json)
finds force-increment sensitivity small and maximum displacement mesh change
.68%, but peak strain changes from **.880% to 1.178%** on .7× refinement
(25.3% normalized difference). Numerical adequacy is false. The isolated fixture
omits the production R.8 root blend and fixes a sharp stub edge; it cannot decide
production strain. It may also consume the nominal 3 mm leaf/liner space.
[The production-root study](../retention_root_study.py) instead crops the actual
full-circumference body 12 mm below both roots, retains blends, guard and liner,
and loads both pads with equal/opposite tractions. The [4 N/pad direct solve](retention/dental_m2b_production_root_direct/root_assessment.json)
completed on 90,470 nodes / 46,254 quadratic tetrahedra. Peak strain .9818% lies
.631 mm above the actual root, not at the annular clamp. However, the sampled
channel lower bound reaches **−.337 mm** at full load: contact-free interpretation
is rejected. At the saved 3.2 N/pad subpath, minimum tooth retraction is
2.2076/2.2173 mm and channel bound remains +.332 mm; strain is .7872%. That is
useful scoped evidence. The [independent 3.2 N run and stronger face witness](retention/production_root.md)
confirm minimum tooth travel 2.2076/2.2173 mm, selected whole quadratic-face
clearance ≥.329 mm, .7872% peak strain and effectively unchanged matched-load
results when load increments decrease from .4 to .32 N. Refinement to 182,772 nodes changes maximum travel 1.36% and retains ≥.293 mm
selected-face channel clearance. Peak strain rises to .8862% (11.17% normalized
change), so it remains mesh-sensitive; no printed strength/recovery is qualified. The original screen is sampled
nodal evidence, not proof of exact deformed-surface clearance or self-contact.

The analytical beam estimate (E800/1200/2200) gives 2.41/3.61/6.62 N per pad for
2.7 mm tip travel and .937% nominal strain. These are uncalibrated preliminary
estimates; the native fixture sensitivity prevents treating them as an effort or
elastic-recovery rating. No unsupported universal 1% PETG limit is adopted. Earlier retained diagnostic
requests carry a provisional `.01` strain screen and may say that narrow screen
passes; this is not material acceptance or a qualified operation result. Current
diagnostic builders use no strain acceptance limit.

## Whole-assembly contact investigation

[Structural driver](../retention_structure.py) builds both actual complete cups
as deformable quadratic tetrahedra. Body butt is clamped; a 6 × 6 mm remote cap
patch is translated in three directions, leaving the intervening shell free to
bend. A side grip and a cropped variant are explicit alternatives. Surface-to-
surface frictionless penalty contact (20,000 N/mm³) includes both hooks, receivers
and guide region; .03 mm penetration limit is a numerical screen. Grip patches
can constrain local rotation: no one fixture is a universal weakest-path model.

The declared material range is E=800–2200 MPa and Poisson .38 as effective
homogeneous isotropic PETG hypotheses. Completed modulus sensitivity is local
traction evidence; whole-contact attempts so far use E1200 only. Actual infill, directional print strength, yield, friction, creep and
fatigue are unknown. These values were not tuned to make M1 fail. Provisional
load scenarios are 5–40 N pull and .2–1 N m bending: hypothetical .3–.6 kg contents
under 2–5 g creates roughly 6–30 N inertia, while snags act independently of mass.
This is a basis for a study range, **not user-approved acceptance criteria**, a
measured contents mass or an impact rating. Those external-load curves have not
yet been qualified and must not be invented from the traction cases.

Uniform 3 mm meshing gives inverted quadratic tetrahedra at small blends. A native
fast-curving probe still leaves 54 invalid elements; it was rejected. Curvature-
resolved mesh controls (.5 mm minimum, 3 mm maximum, 8 points per full circle)
produce positive Jacobians on both actual cups: **310,844 body + 238,519 cap nodes,
305,000 total tetrahedra**. A coarser .8/C6 body mesh fails high-order repair;
it is not accepted merely because its cap meshes. The .12/C12 body mesh has over
1.6 million nodes and is not a useful full-assembly route on this host.

The [full direct solve](retention/dental_m1_full_curv8/result.json) was killed by
the host OOM mechanism (native anonymous resident memory approximately 6.22 GB,
7.7 GB host plus 2 GB swap). It has **no accepted force/displacement frame**.
That is a resource failure, not weak retention. Lower-memory native iterative
solvers were then added to the existing backend and checked on the
[controlled contact benchmark](retention/solver_qualification/README.md): direct,
scaling and Cholesky responses agree, open-gap controls remain unloaded, and
reaction/penetration/strain agree. This small symmetric benchmark does not qualify
large asymmetric sliding contact or cure assembly conditioning.

The seated-start iterative and cropped direct runs were explicitly superseded
after residuals localized to the zero-gap seating rim. The [exact pickup witness](retention/m1_pickup_geometry.json)
confirms that .15 mm unstrained lift closes both 12 mm² retaining lands with no
body/cap intersection; +.01 mm then obstructs. The [native-mesh witness](retention/m1_pickup_native.json)
independently confirms both planar overlaps and .15 mm seating gap in the actual
input. Starting there omits only frictionless zero-work approach, not engagement
deformation. [The full pickup iterative run](retention/dental_m1_full_pickup/result.json)
was interrupted when its owner ended before any accepted increment; it is not a
completed solver comparison or a retention result. Its first Newton iteration
had 11,109 contact springs rather than the seated-start roughly 290,000; spring
counts alone do not establish correct physical contact. Full native input and
convergence records remain retained. The existing FEBio sliding-elastic two-pass augmented-Lagrange route produced
one accepted, balanced [cropped pickup diagnostic](retention/m1_crop_pickup_partial.json):
.05 mm side-grip travel gives .33750 N total axial reaction, split .16568/.17182 N
at the two actual retaining shoulders, with matching receiver forces. Native
maximum penetration is 5.31e-7 mm and independent global balance error 7.15e-10
relative. Load concentrates at shoulder edges while the cap rocks. The actual
free-node internal-force semantics were checked against the exact native source
commit before localizing contact. This is an initial response, not a peak holding
force or completed opening path. The crop was explicitly superseded after this
diagnostic to prioritize the actual full-height memory test.

The [full FEBio PARDISO attempt](retention/dental_m1_full_pickup_febio/result.json),
with both actual shells and all unconstrained deformation retained, reached
1,648,572 equations / 120,015,576 sparse nonzeros. The host killed the native
solver for out-of-memory at about 6.90 GB anonymous resident memory, before any
accepted loaded frame. No other heavy native solve was running. Thus both direct
solver routes have a demonstrated full-assembly resource failure on this host.
The native FEBio FGMRES/ILUT route was investigated as a bounded-memory
alternative. Actual fixtures exposed a native restart-check defect, corrected by
a [small repository plugin using FEBio's official API](../../../physical_analysis/experiments/febio_fgmres/README.md)
against the exact installed build. Same-mesh compression, open-gap and true
iteration-failure controls challenge the correction; its actual-product transfer test was then run through a complete implementation
cycle before requesting further review. The same real cropped assembly was
commanded through .05 mm pickup motion, using the original guarded native mesh,
restart 30, relative tolerance 1e-8, absolute tolerance 1e-12, drop tolerance 1e-5
and ILUT fills 12 then 50. Both reached the native roughly-1000-iteration limit
on the **first linear system**, with no accepted loaded step. Fill 12 ended at
about 588 N residual against a 5.58e-6 N target; fill 50 remained about 558 N.
The [fill-12](retention/dental_m1_crop_ilut_pickup/result.json) and
[fill-50](retention/dental_m1_crop_ilut50_pickup/result.json) runs were deliberately
interrupted after that demonstrated failure, instead of repeating the same
poorly conditioned pickup through automatic smaller-step retries. Their native
failure logs remain retained; interrupted is not completed numerical failure of
the product. The larger fill did not repair the preconditioning problem.

This is the specific technical blocker: a small-fixture-qualified memory-saving
solver does not converge on the actual joint, while both full direct routes
exceed this host's memory. This does not prove that no solver or improved
representation could work. It prevents this investigation from asserting a
full-case barrier or a robust revised retention rating. No geometry, receiver
compliance or rocking was suppressed to manufacture a result, and no residual
criterion was relaxed. Further native solver changes are not included in this
handoff. Neither resource failure nor iteration failure is retention evidence. Until
contact histories, force balance, penetration, increment/mesh sensitivity and
alternative admissible load paths are adequate, **opening resistance, work to
release, receiver spreading, full-size loaded angular stiffness and one-catch
escape remain INCONCLUSIVE/UNKNOWN**. The negative benchmark is not reproduced
merely by showing local inward bending. No favorable fixture result may override
this limit.

## Rejected design and review decisions

[M2A](../m2_design.py) tested matching 7° outward-seating shoulders, 30 mm guide
and .8 mm external receiver reinforcement. Review increased axial play from .4
to .65 mm to avoid binding during pressing. Integral tooth-side gussets corrected
a floating tooth island, but their receiver reliefs created **two floating cap
shelves** over about .27 mm of inverted-print height. Exact layer sections detect
three components. [Retained result](retention/m2_geometry.json) is FAIL despite
390 operation poses passing. [M2A layout](../m2_case.py) is rejected, not a print
alternative. This is why M2B uses flat faces and no side relief/gusset.

Independent engineering review has examined actual source, CAD faces, native
contact surfaces and numerical fields rather than relying on an implementer
summary. Material corrections include fair receiving-land load imprinting,
fixed-stub comparison, distinguishing rigid-pad motion from force-loaded rotation,
retaining unconverged peak strain, validating guide samples in verification,
and rejecting M2A's layer islands. Reviews also preserve the width compromise,
liner/access uncertainty, and the lack of a qualified whole-shell release path.
The reviewer can reject retention qualification independently of software tests.

Final independent review inspected the delivered sources, source-associated
receipts, root comparison and failed native logs. It found no critical defect
blocking this partial research handoff, and explicitly rejected print readiness
or a completed reusable pull-off predictor. No additional solves were requested.

## Review cadence correction

On 2026-10-18 the user corrected the investigation's working method: implement
and verify a coherent end-to-end cycle before asking for independent review.
The repeated early fragment reviews had consumed too much inference quota.
Subsequent work is performed and checked by the implementer, with independent
review reserved for the concrete result. The user permits an honest stop if a
serious attempt encounters a specific technical blocker. This instruction changes
the cadence; it does not turn incomplete contact studies into qualification.

## Reproduction and reusable method

Run from the repository root through the existing execution coordinator. Examples:

```sh
./execute.py model/dental_travel_case/retention_geometry.py /tmp/m1_geometry.json
./execute.py model/dental_travel_case/retention_m2b_checks.py
./execute.py --threads 1 model/dental_travel_case/retention_structure.py eccentric --variant m1 --modulus 1200 --mesh 1.2 --load 12 --receiving-land --root-stub --destination /tmp/m1_stub_repeat
./execute.py --threads 1 model/dental_travel_case/retention_structure.py assembly --variant m1 --modulus 1200 --mesh 3 --mesh-min-size .5 --mesh-curvature-points 8 --travel 2.5 --linear-solver iterative_cholesky --destination /tmp/m1_full_repeat
./execute.py model/dental_travel_case/verification.py --variant slim-m2b
```

Native archives under `retention/` retain input geometry/decks, settings, source
identity, results and relevant logs through `physical_analysis.evidence.retain_run`.
Their compressed inputs reproduce declared fixtures; failed/interrupted records
must not be promoted to successful runs. Native raw nodal fields were used for
the source/hash-guarded deformed-face witness; final displacement is not confused
with the peak of an out-and-back release cycle. Tool identities: CadQuery 2.8.0,
Gmsh 4.15.2, CalculiX 2.21 (startup banner; automatic version field can say unknown),
and FEBio 4.13.0.3ef378562.

[Regression controls](../../../tests/test_dental_retention.py) prove that rounded
obstruction persists beyond useful flat land, guide play can consume pocket
clearance, and removing hooks removes the hook obstruction. Native solver tests
preserve both real contact and a zero-force open gap. These protect detection of
weak evidence rather than hardcoding a winning candidate's holding force.

The demonstrated method now lives in the existing
[retention design guidance](../../../.codex/skills/cadquery-3d-design/references/design-decisions.md),
[method-selection guidance](../../../.codex/skills/engineering-method-selection/SKILL.md)
and [physical-analysis tool documentation](../../../physical_analysis/README.md).
CAD first distinguishes actual flat land/return from corner intersection;
eccentric traction identifies compliance without pretending to measure holding;
full deformable contact is required when receiver movement and long-shell leverage
can change capture. The retained failed baseline and rejected island geometry are
negative controls. A complete reusable pull-off predictor is **not** established.
Shared changes expose existing native mesh/linear-solver choices, with identity,
resource lifecycle and default behavior preserved; no generic retention API,
new scheduler or product score was introduced.
