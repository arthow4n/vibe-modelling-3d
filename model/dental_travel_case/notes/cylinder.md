# Plain threaded dental cylinder

Two PETG parts with a **plain shared interior: no separators, clips or organizers**.
The 264 mm container has an 88 mm bore, 94.4 mm main diameter and 100.4 mm joint
diameter. Sixteen shallow external flutes help grip the two halves. The threaded
joint is near the axial midpoint. Floss may fit spare space but has no dedicated bay.

[Closed inspection assembly](../cylinder_case_assembled.step) is for inspection,
not printing. [Exterior view](../renders/assembled/cylinder_assembled_view_isometric.png).

Print the short pair first, exterior ends on the bed and mouths up. It preserves
the production joint diameter, 3 mm pitch, 0.30 mm radial/0.25 mm axial allowances
and local grip band, while shortening plain walls. Assembled height is 44 mm.
Check thread starting, complete closure, opening effort, whitening/cracking and
wet-hand grip. The test omits full-length wall flex and packing. Clearances are
trial values, not calibrated tolerances.

The full pair prints in the same cup-down orientation, 110 mm apart. After
unscrewing, lift the upper half sufficiently to clear the attached brush. The
conservative packing proxy uses the original 30 mm handle beside a 60 × 44 mm
tube cross-section, with 0.5 mm separation. Usable height is 259.2 mm versus the
reported 255 mm brush. Actual items and tube variation still need a fit check.
Pack dry items; charger excluded. There is no seal or quantified crush rating.

The broad flat ends give bed contact. Flutes taper at their ends; cups flare
smoothly into the larger joint band. Minimum nominal valley walls are 2.2 mm in
the main body and 2.1 mm around the female groove. Thread flanks slope; the female
entry extends two lead turns to clear the finite male thread during opening.

[Verification](cylinder_verification.json) reports PASS for construction,
packing, thread motion and matching slice receipts. Native CAD checked 61 helical
poses at 30° intervals, a subsequent loaded straight lift and obstruction of an
unturned axial pull. This is sampled geometric evidence, not continuous motion,
printed strength or physical fit. Physical use, pressure protection and wet-hand
grip remain UNKNOWN. Run the shared entry with
`./execute.py model/dental_travel_case/verification.py --variant plain-cylinder`.

Both [full-layout](slicer_review/cylinder_case.json) and
[test-layout](slicer_review/cylinder_thread_test.json) smoke slices completed in
OrcaSlicer 2.4.2, centered on the Qidi Q2C 270 × 270 × 256 mm bed, with no notices
and no support generated by its automatic support probe. The diagnostic setup is
PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, two walls, 7% adaptive cubic, 3 mm outer brim,
no skirt. Temperatures, flow and speeds are diagnostic, not calibrated production
settings. The STL checks do not validate Orca's separate GUI STEP import.
No changed cylinder geometry followed those paired exports and slices.
